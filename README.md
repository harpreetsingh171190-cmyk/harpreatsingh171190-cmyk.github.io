## 🛠️ Focus Areas & Domains
- **Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PIML):** Developing neural network surrogates grounded in analytical continuum mechanics.
- **CFD & Aerodynamic Surrogates:** Data-driven acceleration of high-fidelity aerodynamic and flow simulations.
- **Thermal-Fluid Analysis:** Parametric modeling and optimization of heat exchangers and thermal dissipation systems.

---

## 🚀 Featured Projects

1. **[Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) for 2D Structural Stress](https://github.com/harpreetsingh171190-cmyk/pinn-fea-stress-surrogate)**
   - Predicts 2D Von Mises stress fields and notch stress concentration factors ($K_t$) under uniaxial tension.
   - Enforces Kirsch equations and Peterson finite-width boundary constraints into loss formulations.

2. **Airfoil Aerodynamic CFD Surrogate Modeling**
   - Rapid estimation of lift ($C_L$) and drag ($C_D$) coefficients across varying angles of attack and Reynolds numbers.

3. **Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger Optimization**
   - Parametric design space exploration for heat transfer rates and pressure drop minimization.

4. **Heat Sink Thermal Performance Analysis**
   - Numerical and surrogate-driven temperature distribution modeling for conjugate heat transfer.
